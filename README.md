Bank Marketing Data Analysis
Project Overview
This project analyses customer and campaign data from a bank marketing dataset to identify the key factors influencing term deposit subscriptions. The project involves data cleaning, preprocessing, exploratory data analysis (EDA), and visualisation to uncover customer behaviour patterns and generate business insights.

Objective
To understand customer characteristics, campaign effectiveness, and economic factors that impact subscription decisions, helping banks improve future marketing strategies.

Dataset Information
Dataset: Bank Marketing Dataset
Rows: 39,188
Columns: 21
Target Variable: y (Subscription Status)

Key Findings
Most customers did not subscribe to term deposits.
Call duration significantly influenced subscription success.
Cellular communication produced better response rates than telephone calls.
Previous successful campaign outcomes increased future subscription likelihood.
Customer demographics and education levels affected subscription behaviour.
Conclusion
The analysis provides valuable insights into customer behaviour and marketing campaign performance, enabling banks to make data-driven decisions and improve campaign effectiveness.
